Item states

Every lot is in exactly one state. Badges always pair a word and an icon with the color.

GoodSoonCautionInsp.Exp.
▲ printed date
Good

Before the printed date, outside the notice window.Counts toward both ends of the range.

Use soon

Within 30 days of the printed date. A nudge, not a warning.Counts toward both ends. Notice window set per household.

Caution

Past best-by, inside the extension window. Eat or rotate first.Counts toward the high end only.

Inspect

Final 20% of the extension window. Check for bulging, rust or leaks.Counts toward the high end only.

Expired

Past the extension window, or past any use-by date.Not counted. Red is used only here and for a shortfall below 3 days.

Use-by items skip Caution and Inspect: Good → Use soon → Expired. Kits use their own words: Complete, Use soon, Short, Missing.
